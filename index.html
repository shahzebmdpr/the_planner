ℹ️ How to get your free Upstash Redis credentials (Takes 30 seconds)
  1. Create a free account at console.upstash.com (no credit card required).
  2. Click "Create Database", choose Redis, pick the region closest to you, and click Create.
  3. Scroll down to the "REST API" section on your database dashboard.
  4. Click on the JavaScript / Fetch tab.
  5. Copy the UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL and UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN and paste them above!
  6. Share the invite link with your friend so they connect in one click without needing to configure anything.
