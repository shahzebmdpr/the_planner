ℹ️ How to get your free Upstash Redis credentials
  1. Create a free account at console.upstash.com.
  2. Click "Create Database", choose Redis, pick the region closest to you, and click Create.
  3. Scroll down to the "REST API" section on your database dashboard.
  4. Click on the JavaScript / Fetch tab.
  5. Copy the UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_URL and UPSTASH_REDIS_REST_TOKEN.
